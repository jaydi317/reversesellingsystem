What actually happens

Three steps. None of them are a sales call.

  1. You tell us where to look.

    Name, email, phone. Thirty seconds. That's the whole form.

  2. We do the research.

    The same research that goes into the letters we send business owners — how your phone gets answered, what happens after someone reaches out, what your competitors are doing that you aren't.

  3. We walk you through what we found.

    A short, free conversation. You keep everything we found either way. If there's a fit after that, we'll say so. If there isn't, we'll say that too.
