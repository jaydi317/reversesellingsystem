The Group Posting Play

How we fill the room from other people's Facebook groups. Sept 10 event. Link everywhere is reversesellingsystem.com/meetup.

The rule

Never post cold. Ask the admin first. An admin's yes turns an ad into an endorsement.
Groups delete drive-by promo posts. But when the admin approves or shares it, the group treats it as theirs. That is how the successful meetups actually filled: one to one invites plus group endorsements. The event page does the selling; the post's only job is the click.

The 3-step loop, repeat per group

1
DM the admin
Use the admin script below. Wait for a yes.
2
Post
Use the group post below, or let the admin share it themselves, which is even better.
3
Work the comments
Reply to every comment within the hour, then DM anyone who comments "interested" with the link. Comments are leads.

Admin DM script

Paul sends these
"Hey, I help run a free night for local business owners next Thursday at Launch Fishers. We fix real owners' problems with AI live on a big screen, nothing is sold, everyone leaves with free tools. I'd rather ask you than just post it. Would you be open to sharing it with the group, or is it OK if I post? Happy to hold seats for your members. Here's the page so you can check it's legit: reversesellingsystem.com/meetup"

The group post

Goes up only after the yes
"Admin said it was OK to share this. Next Thursday night there's a free event for business owners at Launch Fishers. Real owners bring real problems and AI fixes them live on the big screen. Nothing is sold. Everyone leaves with ready made AI helpers for their business, plus food and drinks. If you run a business in Hamilton County, seats are capped: reversesellingsystem.com/meetup"
Post as yourself, not a page. Add one real sentence of your own at the top about why you're going. Personal beats polished.

Where to run it

  • Fishers community groups
  • HSE parents and neighborhood groups
  • Carmel small business groups
  • Noblesville and Westfield community groups
  • Indy entrepreneur and small business groups
  • Any chamber or BNI adjacent group either of us is already in
  • Niche owner groups (contractors, salons, restaurants, real estate)
Rule of thumb: groups where owners hang out beat groups about AI.

The don'ts

  • Don't post the same wording in every group at the same hour. Facebook flags it: change the first sentence each time.
  • Don't argue with a skeptic in comments. Invite them: "come see it live, it's free."
  • Don't post in AI groups. They're not owners.
  • Don't post without the admin yes. One deleted post can get you muted in the group.
