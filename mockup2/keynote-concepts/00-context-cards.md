# Reverse Selling + Jimmy Magic — keynote concept context cards

## 01 — The Gravity Switch

- Placement: Reverse Selling big-idea introduction.
- Entry: Selling means finding better ways to push a message toward a prospect.
- Job: Change the perceived direction of movement.
- Exit: The buyer becomes the moving object; the business changes the field.
- Danger: Do not imply passive marketing or that attraction requires no system.

## 02 — The Hidden Frame

- Placement: Jimmy Magic explanation after the audience sees his raw persuasive skill.
- Entry: Facts persuade because the facts are inherently compelling.
- Job: Reveal that sequence and framing control what the facts mean.
- Exit: The same facts can create a different decision when the hidden frame changes.
- Danger: Do not imply manipulation or distortion of facts.

## 03 — The Rosetta Engine

- Placement: Jimmy Magic mechanism section.
- Entry: Complex ideas must be simplified until important nuance is lost.
- Job: Show translation across story, science, and desire.
- Exit: Complexity can become obvious without becoming smaller or less true.
- Danger: Do not imply scientific validation where only analogy exists.

## 04 — The Guided Epiphany

- Placement: Conclusion-led selling section.
- Entry: Persuasion succeeds when the claim is stated clearly enough.
- Job: Show the buyer completing the final connection.
- Exit: Jimmy supplies the evidence path; the buyer owns the conclusion.
- Danger: The route must feel guided, not predetermined or coercive.

## 05 — Trust Before the Ask

- Placement: Results-in-advance or Invisible Funnel section.
- Entry: The offer must create enough desire to overcome risk.
- Job: Put delivered value and certainty before the offer.
- Exit: The invitation arrives after belief, so the choice feels safe and earned.
- Danger: Do not promise a guaranteed financial result.

## 06 — The Moment Map

- Placement: Jimmy Magic keystone or authority section.
- Entry: Jimmy Magic is charisma, improvisation, or unexplained instinct.
- Job: Reveal real-time synthesis of story, status, safety, proof, and choice.
- Exit: The magic is seeing hidden signals and sequencing the next move.
- Danger: Do not reduce the human skill to literal software automation.

## 07 — The Decision Runway

- Placement: Reverse Selling closing mechanism.
- Entry: Closing is a final burst of persuasion at the end of a sales process.
- Job: Show that each belief must land before the offer does.
- Exit: The close is natural because recognition, understanding, safety, and readiness are already in place.
- Danger: Do not imply every buyer should or will choose yes.
