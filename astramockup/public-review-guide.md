Start at https://reversesellingsystem.com/astramockup/

This page gathers the letter, its history, and the tools that make its promise concrete. It is for feedback on a proposed event experience. V3 is the current candidate. The live meetup registration page remains separate.

## The job

Get the right local business owners to give Jimmy and Paul two hours on Thursday, September 10, 2026. They should understand what will happen, want the result, trust the invitation, and know the next step.

A good attendee may be new to AI or already using it. The common thread is a real business they want to improve. Customer choice, growth, time, and pride all matter. The early warm-network registrations do not define this market.

## Review in this order

1. Read V3 on your phone without stopping to edit. Note the first place you drift.
2. Compare the original, Jason's version, Astra V1, and the V2 bridge. Read the change history.
3. Use one customer-message tool and one practical tool with the supplied fictional example. Check whether the promised head start is real.
4. Look through the proposed 14-slide event sequence and the phone rehearsal. The poll, question, and notes demo stays on your device; it does not connect a live audience.
5. Return three decisions: KEEP, REWORK, KILL. Quote the exact line or name the exact tool. Explain the owner reaction you want instead.

## Three useful reviewer perspectives

**Paul:** Does this sound like the business you and Jimmy are building together? Is your operating experience told accurately? Which real example would you be proud to lead? What should the owner be able to do before that demonstration ends?

**Jason:** Where does the page make the next step easy? Which parts of your rebuild deserve to return? Does the calculator help the reader make a decision? Where does the page repeat itself or slow down?

**Daegan:** What belief has the reader not reached before the next claim asks for it? Where should the reader discover the idea for themselves? Which section makes the free Thursday night feel worth attending?

Everyone can comment on everything. These are starting points, not assigned lanes.
