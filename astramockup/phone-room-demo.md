## Exact host actions

1. On the host computer, enter the private host key and click **Open host desk**.
2. Confirm the room selector says **Rehearsal**. If it says **Thursday**, switch back to **Rehearsal** before doing anything else.
3. On the host desk, click **Welcome + QR**. The stage should show the rehearsal QR and the words **REHEARSAL ROOM**.
4. In the host card **01 · MAKE THE ROOM MOVE**, click **Where are you with AI?**. This launches poll `hello`.
5. Confirm the poll card now says **Close voting**. If it says **Reopen**, click **Reopen** once.
6. On Phone 1 tap **I have barely tried it**. On Phone 2 tap **I build with it**.
7. On the host computer click **Close voting**, then click **Reveal results**.
8. Look at the big screen. It should show two votes, the two selected choices, and bars that match them. Say: **“The phones are not decoration. They change what the room sees.”**
9. Click **Which company would you call?**. This launches poll `message`.
10. Show these fictional authored drafts on the big screen, then have one phone choose A and one choose B:
    - **A · Quality. Integrity. Excellence.**
    - **B · AC quit? Tell us what happened. We’ll explain the next step and confirm the visit price.**
    Close and reveal again.
11. Click **What should we work on next?**. This launches poll `next`. Let both phones choose different answers. Reveal and say: **“Now the room chooses the next job.”**
12. Click the exact tool **Teach your first AI helper** in the host tool list. Both phones should see it in the live room and can open it under **My tools**.
13. Click **Would you actually use what we just made?**. This launches poll `verdict`. Have one person choose **Yes, with a few changes** and the other choose **Show me how it fits my business**. Reveal and ask: **“What would you check before using it?”**
14. Finish by clicking **What will you try on Friday?**. This launches poll `friday`. Each person chooses one answer, saves a Friday plan, and can download it.

## Paul’s short narration

Use these lines while the actions happen:

> “There is no right answer here. I want to know what the room actually looks like.”

> “You are not voting on which sentence sounds fancier. You are voting on which company you would call.”

> “This is where I want to listen. Which example would you most like to see?”

> “Would you actually use that?”

> “A tool earns its place by making one job clearer, not by sounding impressive.”

> “Pick one small thing you can try Friday morning, before the week gets loud again.”

## What the reviewers should notice

- Each person has an independent phone session.
- The stage does not reveal votes until the host chooses **Reveal results**.
- The host can change the poll without asking the audience to reload.
- A tool can be sent to every phone while the stage stays focused on the live conversation.
- Questions are reviewed by the host before they appear on the stage.
- The Friday plan gives the audience a next action rather than a vague promise.

## If anything fails during the review

Use the paper cards. The meaningful experience is still intact: answer a question, compare a draft, choose a job, judge the result, and write a Friday step. Do not switch to the live room during a rehearsal test.
