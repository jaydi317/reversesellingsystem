## What changed

- A shared hosted room replaces the single-computer prototype. Separate rehearsal and Thursday rooms.
- Five audience decisions: starting point, customer message, next job, usefulness, and Friday action.
- A large stage view with a real QR, changing vote counts, held results, and moderated questions.
- Paul’s private question desk and clear moments for him to open, diagnose, challenge, and close.
- Twelve owner tools. Personalize an exercise, copy it into an AI chat, reopen the saved instructions, or download them with a Friday plan.
- Five fictional host practice cases, fourteen stage slides, a rehearsal script, and six printable fallback pages.
- A separate free 15-minute calendar event. The old 30-minute event and /call route are preserved.
- Eight-second request timeouts, retry safeguards, private plans, saved phone work, and offline static caching.

The tools prepare instructions; they do not secretly call an AI or connect to business systems. The live AI demonstration happens in Jimmy and Paul’s AI chat. A saved example is always labeled.

## What is verified

The first public release passed 34 API checks with 45 independent simulated phone sessions. A further sustained check completed 450 of 450 reads from 45 simulated readers, with no errors. These are server tests, not a test of 45 physical phones at Launch Fishers.

The new booking route showed an actual 15-minute slot and the final name/email form. A self-only test booking was accepted, produced a Google Calendar reference and a meeting link, then was cancelled and read back as cancelled. Inbox delivery and arrival on a physical phone have not been verified.

## What still needs a rehearsal

Scan from the back row, use two real phones, test venue Wi-Fi, test the host’s cellular fallback, and rehearse the projector. Do not assume a personal hotspot can carry 45 phones. If the connection stalls for 30 seconds, use the matching printed card and keep the show moving.
