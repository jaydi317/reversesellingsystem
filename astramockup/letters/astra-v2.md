## Take your Friday-morning head start home.

You won't need to scribble down every word.

You'll get our notes, the drawings, and instructions you can use again. Open them, pick a job, and follow the steps.

**Get inside your customer's head.** Work out what people need to hear before they're ready to call, book, or buy. Separate what you know from what you still need to ask.

**Give your helper a job.** Copy-and-paste instructions for follow-ups, customer notes, review replies, and other everyday work. Add your details. Check the result. Use them again.

**Make your first plan.** Pick one job, decide what a good result looks like, and write down the next step. “I'm not sure where to start” is a perfectly good starting point.

**Skip the next rabbit hole.** A quick guide to deciding whether an AI tool is worth trying for the job you actually need done.

These are instructions and exercises you can use with tools such as ChatGPT or Claude. Connecting AI to your phone, inbox, or other systems takes separate setup. Some tools have their own fees.
